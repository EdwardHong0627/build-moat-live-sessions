# Requirements — Raw (Identified)

> Source: `PROMPT.md` · Generated by requirement-identifier · 2026-05-29

## Summary
The document specifies a Q&A bot over a small Markdown knowledge base, exposed via an HTTP API, with answers grounded in indexed documents and cited back to the source Markdown. It offers two interchangeable retrieval strategies (a plain-Markdown BM25 KB and a vector RAG path), defines a verification suite of `/health`, `/index`, and `/chat` behaviors, and lists optional stretch goals. I identified 22 functional requirements (core plus stretch) and 5 constraints, plus several open questions around thresholds and persistence semantics.
